# Thomas George

Co. G, 31st U.S. Colored Infantry · died 1897-11-05

## Life

Thomas George lies in grave K2 of the Prescott Post lot. His stone names his unit as Company G of the 31st U.S. Colored Infantry, and gives his death as November 5, 1897. [1]

No Thomas George was found in the index for the 31st U.S. Colored Infantry (there is one in the 110th); the spelling on his service record may differ. [2]

A 2022 list of Civil War veterans buried at North Burial Ground gives his death as 1897. [3]

The 31st U.S. Colored Infantry was organized on Hart's Island, New York, in April 1864. [4] The flag on this page is New York's for that reason.

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.

## What we don't know

- Did he enlist in Connecticut? Men of the 30th Connecticut (Colored) were merged into the 31st in May 1864. If his service record shows it, the flag here should be Connecticut's. [4]
- When and where did he enlist, and what did his company do? His compiled military service record (National Archives, RG 94) will say.
- Did he or his widow apply for a pension? The General Index to Pension Files (NARA T288/T289) has not yet been searched for him.
- Where was he born, and how did he come to Providence?
- Where did he live and work in Providence? The city directories and the censuses of 1870–1920 have not yet been read for him.
- Did he marry, and did he leave children?
- When was he buried here, and what does the City's interment record say? The City lot record has not yet been matched.
