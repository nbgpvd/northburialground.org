# Thomas George

Co. G, 31st U.S. Colored Infantry · died 1897-11-05

## Life

Thomas George lies in grave K2 of the Prescott Post lot. His stone names his unit as Company G of the 31st U.S. Colored Infantry, and gives his death as November 5, 1897. [1]

No Thomas George was found in the index for the 31st U.S. Colored Infantry (there is one in the 110th); the spelling on his service record may differ. [2]

A 2022 list of Civil War veterans buried at North Burial Ground gives his death as 1897. [3]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.
