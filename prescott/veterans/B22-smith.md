# CHARLES H. SMITH

Co. C, 33rd Massachusetts Infantry

## Life

Charles H. Smith lies in grave B22 of the Prescott Post lot. His stone names his unit as Company C of the 33rd Massachusetts Infantry. No date of death has been read from it. [1]

The National Park Service's index of Civil War soldiers lists Smith, Charles H., 33rd Massachusetts Infantry, Company F, private in and out, microfilm M544 roll 37. [2] The card gives Company F; the stone reads C. Which Charles H. Smith this is has not been established.

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.

## What we don't know

- The PV001 list has a Charles H. Smith of the 33rd Massachusetts who died in 1890. That date does not fit this row, where the burials run from 1915 to 1919. The stone's date line has not been read.
- The surname was first read as Small; an enhanced photograph reads SMITH.
- When and where did he enlist, and what did his company do? His compiled military service record (National Archives, RG 94) will say.
- Did he or his widow apply for a pension? The General Index to Pension Files (NARA T288/T289) has not yet been searched for him.
- Where was he born, and how did he come to Providence?
- Where did he live and work in Providence? The city directories and the censuses of 1870–1920 have not yet been read for him.
- Did he marry, and did he leave children?
- When was he buried here, and what does the City's interment record say? The City lot record has not yet been matched.
