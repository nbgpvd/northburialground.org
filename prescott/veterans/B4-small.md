# ROBERT W. SMALL

Co. I, 2nd Rhode Island Volunteers · died 1918-11-04

## Life

Robert W. Small lies in grave B4 of the Prescott Post lot. His stone names his unit as Company I of the 2nd Rhode Island Volunteers, and gives his death as November 4, 1918. [1]

The National Park Service's index of Civil War soldiers lists Small, Robert W., 2nd Rhode Island Infantry, Company I, private in, first lieutenant and regimental quartermaster out, field and staff, microfilm M555 roll 6. [2] The stone gives no rank.

A 2022 list of Civil War veterans buried at North Burial Ground gives his death as 1918. [3]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.
