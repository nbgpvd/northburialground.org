# [RI]CHARD H. [F]REEMA[N]

Co. K, 29th Connecticut Volunteers · died 1917-05-08

## Life

Richard H. Freeman lies in grave B10 of the Prescott Post lot. His stone names his unit as Company K of the 29th Connecticut Volunteers, and gives his death as May 8, 1917. [1]

No Richard Freeman was found in the index for the 29th Connecticut Infantry; the index has other men named Freeman in that regiment, and his record may be filed under another spelling. [2]

A 2022 list of Civil War veterans buried at North Burial Ground gives his death as 1917. [3]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.

## What we don't know

- The name was read from an enhanced photograph; the first letters of the given name and of the surname are worn. The PV001 list records a Richard H. Freeman, Company K, 29th Connecticut Volunteers, died 1917, which agrees with the stone.
- His service in the 29th rests on his stone and the PV001 list until his service record is found.
- When and where did he enlist, and what did his company do? His compiled military service record (National Archives, RG 94) will say.
- Did he or his widow apply for a pension? The General Index to Pension Files (NARA T288/T289) has not yet been searched for him.
- Where was he born, and how did he come to Providence?
- Where did he live and work in Providence? The city directories and the censuses of 1870–1920 have not yet been read for him.
- Did he marry, and did he leave children?
- When was he buried here, and what does the City's interment record say? The City lot record has not yet been matched.
