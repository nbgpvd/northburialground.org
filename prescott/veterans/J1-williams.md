# HENRY B. WILLIAMS

Corporal, Co. M, 14th Rhode Island Heavy Artillery · died 1895-01-27

## Life

Henry B. Williams lies in grave J1 of the Prescott Post lot. His stone names his unit as Company M of the 14th Rhode Island Heavy Artillery, and gives his death as January 27, 1895. [1]

The National Park Service's index of Civil War soldiers, which files the 14th Rhode Island under its later name, the 11th U.S. Colored Heavy Artillery, lists Williams, Henry B., 11th U.S. Colored Heavy Artillery, Company M, private in, corporal out, microfilm M589 roll 95. [2]

He appears in the roster of the regiment's 1898 history, page 258. [3]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.
