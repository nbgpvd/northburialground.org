# JOHN HAWKESWORTH

Co. E, 1st Massachusetts Volunteers · died 1916

## Life

John Hawkesworth lies in grave B19 of the Prescott Post lot. His stone names his unit as Company E of the 1st Massachusetts Volunteers, and gives his death as a day in June of a year in the 1910s; the PV001 list gives 1916. [1]

The National Park Service's index of Civil War soldiers lists Hawkesworth, John (filed under Hawksworth), 1st Massachusetts Heavy Artillery, Company E, private in and out, microfilm M544 roll 18. [2] The stone says 1st Massachusetts Volunteers; the card says heavy artillery. His service record will say which.

A 2022 list of Civil War veterans buried at North Burial Ground gives his death as 1916. [3]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.
