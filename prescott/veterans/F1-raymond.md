# [J]AMES S. RAYMOND

5th Massachusetts Cavalry

## Life

James S. Raymond lies in grave F1 of the Prescott Post lot. His stone names his unit as 5th Massachusetts Cavalry, and gives his death as May 23, in a year that fell outside the photograph. [1]

The National Park Service's index of Civil War soldiers and sailors has not yet been searched for him.

A 2022 list of Civil War veterans buried at North Burial Ground gives his death as 1905. [2]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.
