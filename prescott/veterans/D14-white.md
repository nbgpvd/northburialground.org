# Jacob White

Co. F, 2nd U.S. Colored Cavalry

## Life

Jacob White lies in grave D14 of the Prescott Post lot. The plot survey records his stone as naming his unit as Company F of the 2nd U.S. Colored Cavalry. No date of death has been read from the stone. [1]

The National Park Service's index of Civil War soldiers lists White, Jacob, 2nd U.S. Colored Cavalry, Company F, private in and out, microfilm M589 roll 93. [2]

A 2022 list of Civil War veterans buried at North Burial Ground gives his death as 1910. [3]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.
