# ROBERT S. JOHNSON

Co. K, 1st U.S. Colored Infantry · died 1897-03-16

## Life

Robert S. Johnson lies in grave K6 of the Prescott Post lot. His stone names his unit as Company K of the 1st U.S. Colored Infantry, and gives his death as March 16, 1897. [1]

The National Park Service's index of Civil War soldiers lists Johnson, Robert S., 1st U.S. Colored Infantry, Company K, private in and out, microfilm M589 roll 48. [2]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.
