# MARSHALL KING

Co. A, 23rd U.S. Colored Infantry · died 1895-01-14

## Life

Marshall King lies in grave J4 of the Prescott Post lot. His stone names his unit as Company A of the 23rd U.S. Colored Infantry, and gives his death as January 14, 1895. [1]

The National Park Service's index of Civil War soldiers lists King, Marshal, 23rd U.S. Colored Infantry, Company A, private in and out, microfilm M589 roll 51. [2]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.
