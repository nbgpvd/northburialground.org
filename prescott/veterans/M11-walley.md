# WILLIAM W. WALLEY

Co. I, 54th U.S. Colored Infantry

## Life

William W. Walley lies in grave M11 of the Prescott Post lot. His stone names his unit as Company I of the 54th U.S. Colored Infantry, and gives his death as 1901 according to the PV001 list; the stone's date line has not been read. [1]

No William Walley or Whalley was found in the index for the 54th U.S. Colored Infantry; the spelling on his service record may differ. [2]

The 54th U.S. Colored Infantry was raised in Arkansas in 1863 as the 2nd Arkansas Infantry (African Descent), and took its new name on March 11, 1864. [4] The flag on this page is Arkansas's for that reason.

A 2022 list of Civil War veterans buried at North Burial Ground gives his death as 1901. [3]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.
