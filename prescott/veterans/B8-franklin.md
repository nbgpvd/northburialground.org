# [MA]SON B. FRANKLIN

Co. H, 2nd Rhode Island Volunteers · died 1918-01-30

## Life

Mason B. Franklin lies in grave B8 of the Prescott Post lot. His stone names his unit as Company H of the 2nd Rhode Island Volunteers, and gives his death as January 30, 1918. [1]

The National Park Service's index of Civil War soldiers lists Franklin, Mason B., 2nd Rhode Island Infantry, Company H, private in and out, microfilm M555 roll 3. [2]

A 2022 list of Civil War veterans buried at North Burial Ground gives his death as 1918. [3]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.
