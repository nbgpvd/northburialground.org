# [WI]LL[IAM] E. SMITH

Co. H, 11th Rhode Island Volunteers · died 1918-03-27

## Life

William E. Smith lies in grave B23 of the Prescott Post lot. His stone names his unit as Company H of the 11th Rhode Island Volunteers. No date of death has been read from it. [1]

The National Park Service's index of Civil War soldiers lists Smith, William E., 11th Rhode Island Infantry, Company H, private in and out, microfilm M555 roll 6. [2]

A 2022 list of Civil War veterans buried at North Burial Ground gives his death as 1918. [3]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.

## What we don't know

- The stone is badly worn. Only LL, E. SMITH and the unit line can be read; "William" comes from the PV001 list and the index, both of which have a William E. Smith in Company H of the 11th Rhode Island. The identification is probable, not certain.
- The PV001 list gives his death as March 27, 1918, which is later than his neighbors' (1915–1916). The stone's date line has not been read.
- When and where did he enlist, and what did his company do? His compiled military service record (National Archives, RG 94) will say.
- Did he or his widow apply for a pension? The General Index to Pension Files (NARA T288/T289) has not yet been searched for him.
- Where was he born, and how did he come to Providence?
- Where did he live and work in Providence? The city directories and the censuses of 1870–1920 have not yet been read for him.
- Did he marry, and did he leave children?
- When was he buried here, and what does the City's interment record say? The City lot record has not yet been matched.
