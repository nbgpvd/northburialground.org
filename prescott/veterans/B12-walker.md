# JOHN T. WALKER

Co. C, 2nd US Cavalry · died 1917-01-30

## Life

John T. Walker lies in grave B12 of the Prescott Post lot. His stone names his unit as Company C of the 2nd US Cavalry, and gives his death as January 30, 1917. [1]

No John T. Walker was found in the index for the 2nd U.S. Cavalry, whose regular-army records the index covers only in part. [2]

A 2022 list of Civil War veterans buried at North Burial Ground gives his death as 1917. [3]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.
