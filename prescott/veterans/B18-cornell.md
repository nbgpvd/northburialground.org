# JOSEPH H. CORNELL

Co. C, 11th Rhode Island Volunteers

## Life

Joseph H. Cornell lies in grave B18 of the Prescott Post lot. His stone names his unit as Company C of the 11th Rhode Island Volunteers. No date of death has been read from it. [1]

The National Park Service's index of Civil War soldiers lists Cornell, Joseph H., 11th Rhode Island Infantry, Company G, private in and out, with the note "See also 3 R.I. Cavy.", microfilm M555 roll 2. [2] The company letter on the stone was read as C; the index gives G.

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.

## What we don't know

- The PV001 list has a Joseph H. Cornell, 11th Rhode Island, Company G, who died on March 30, 1905. That date does not fit this row, where the burials run from 1915 to 1919. Is this the same man? The stone's date line, now under earth, is the test.
- The 11th Rhode Island was a nine-month regiment of 1862–63; the index note points to later service in the 3rd Rhode Island Cavalry.
- When and where did he enlist, and what did his company do? His compiled military service record (National Archives, RG 94) will say.
- Did he or his widow apply for a pension? The General Index to Pension Files (NARA T288/T289) has not yet been searched for him.
- Where was he born, and how did he come to Providence?
- Where did he live and work in Providence? The city directories and the censuses of 1870–1920 have not yet been read for him.
- Did he marry, and did he leave children?
- When was he buried here, and what does the City's interment record say? The City lot record has not yet been matched.
