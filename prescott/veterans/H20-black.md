# George H. Black

Sergeant, Co. A, 14th Rhode Island Heavy Artillery · died 1887-05-14

## Life

George H. Black lies in grave H20 of the Prescott Post lot. The plot survey records his stone as naming his unit as Company A of the 14th Rhode Island Heavy Artillery, and gives his death as May 14, 1887. [1]

The National Park Service's index of Civil War soldiers, which files the 14th Rhode Island under its later name, the 11th U.S. Colored Heavy Artillery, lists Black, George H., 11th U.S. Colored Heavy Artillery, Company A, corporal in, sergeant out, microfilm M589 roll 7. [2]

He appears in the roster of the regiment's 1898 history, page 156 and 10. [3]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.
