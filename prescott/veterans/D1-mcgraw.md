# Thomas McGraw

Co. A, 9th Maine Volunteers

## Life

Thomas McGraw lies in grave D1 of the Prescott Post lot. The plot survey records his stone as naming his unit as Company A of the 9th Maine Volunteers. No date of death has been read from the stone. [1]

The National Park Service's index of Civil War soldiers lists McGraw, Thomas H., 9th Maine Infantry, Company A, private in, corporal out, microfilm M543 roll 14. [2]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.
