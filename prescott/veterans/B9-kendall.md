# CHARLES H. KENDALL

Co. B, 2nd Rhode Island Volunteers · died 1917-10-22

## Life

Charles H. Kendall lies in grave B9 of the Prescott Post lot. His stone names his unit as Company B of the 2nd Rhode Island Volunteers, and gives his death as October 22, 1917. [1]

The National Park Service's index of Civil War soldiers lists Kendall, Charles (also filed as Charles H. Kendall), 2nd Rhode Island Infantry, Companies E and B, private in and out, microfilm M555 roll 4. [2]

A 2022 list of Civil War veterans buried at North Burial Ground gives his death as 1917. [3]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.
