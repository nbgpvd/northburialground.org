# WILLIAM H. SMITH

Co. H, 29th Connecticut Volunteers · died 1912-04-24

## Life

William H. Smith lies in grave C21 of the Prescott Post lot. His stone names his unit as Company H of the 29th Connecticut Volunteers, and gives his death as April 24, 1912. [1]

The National Park Service's index of Civil War soldiers lists Smith, William H., 29th Connecticut Infantry (Colored), Company H, private in and out, microfilm M589 roll 81, African American Civil War Memorial plaque E-148. [2] The index has other William Smiths and William H. Smiths in the regiment; this card is the one in Company H, the company the stone names.

A 2022 list of Civil War veterans buried at North Burial Ground gives his death as 1912. [3]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.

## What we don't know

- With so common a name, the regiment and company on the stone are the guide to his service record.
- When and where did he enlist, and what did his company do? His compiled military service record (National Archives, RG 94) will say.
- Did he or his widow apply for a pension? The General Index to Pension Files (NARA T288/T289) has not yet been searched for him.
- Where was he born, and how did he come to Providence?
- Where did he live and work in Providence? The city directories and the censuses of 1870–1920 have not yet been read for him.
- Did he marry, and did he leave children?
- When was he buried here, and what does the City's interment record say? The City lot record has not yet been matched.
