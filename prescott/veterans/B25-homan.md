# John G. Homan

Co. F, 15th New York Volunteers · died 1915-11-28

## Life

John G. Homan lies in grave B25 of the Prescott Post lot. The plot survey records his stone as naming his unit as Company F of the 15th New York Volunteers, and gives his death as November 28, 1915. [1]

The National Park Service's index of Civil War soldiers lists Homan, John G., 15th New York Infantry (National Guard, thirty days' service, 1864), Company F, private in and out, microfilm M551 roll 66. [2]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.
