# Adam D. Lee

Corporal, Co. L, 14th Rhode Island Heavy Artillery

## Life

Adam D. Lee lies in grave D7 of the Prescott Post lot. The plot survey records his stone as naming his unit as Company L of the 14th Rhode Island Heavy Artillery. No date of death has been read from the stone. [1]

The National Park Service's index of Civil War soldiers, which files the 14th Rhode Island under its later name, the 11th U.S. Colored Heavy Artillery, lists Lee, Adam, 11th U.S. Colored Heavy Artillery, Company L, private in, corporal out, microfilm M589 roll 53. [2]

He appears in the roster of the regiment's 1898 history, page 248. [3]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.
