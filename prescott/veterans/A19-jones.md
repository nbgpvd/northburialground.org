# ALEXANDER JONES

Private, Co. G, 2nd U.S. Colored Infantry · died 1927-01-11

## Life

Alexander Jones lies in grave A19 of the Prescott Post lot. His stone names his unit as Company G of the 2nd U.S. Colored Infantry, and gives his death as January 11, 1927. [1]

The National Park Service's index of Civil War soldiers lists Jones, Alexander, 2nd U.S. Colored Cavalry, Company G, private in and out, microfilm M589 roll 49. [2] The stone says infantry; the only Alexander Jones the index places in Company G of a 2nd U.S. Colored regiment is in the cavalry. No Alexander Jones was found in the 2nd U.S. Colored Infantry. Which regiment he served in is unresolved.

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.
