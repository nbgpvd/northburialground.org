# JAMES B. BERRY

Co. A, 39th U.S. Colored Infantry

## Life

James B. Berry lies in grave M23 of the Prescott Post lot. His stone names his unit as Company A of the 39th U.S. Colored Infantry, and gives his death as an April day of a year now illegible on the stone. [1]

No James B. Berry was found in the index for the 39th U.S. Colored Infantry. The index has a James B. Berry in the 128th U.S. Colored Infantry, not yet checked. [2]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.
