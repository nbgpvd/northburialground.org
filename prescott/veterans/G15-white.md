# THOMAS J. WH[I]T[E]



## Life

Thomas J. White lies in grave G15 of the Prescott Post lot. His stone is badly weathered: the name can be read, the line that named his unit has worn away, and it gives his death as October 7 of a year in the 1870s. [1]

The index has two Thomas Whites in Rhode Island units (Battery G, 1st Rhode Island Light Artillery, attached from a New York battery; and Company D, 9th Rhode Island) and none with the initial J. With the unit line gone from his stone, neither can be matched to him. [2]

A 2022 list of Civil War veterans buried at North Burial Ground has a Thomas J. White, born about 1820, who died in 1876. [3]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.

## What we don't know

- The stone is badly weathered and the unit line has worn away entirely. The PV001 list has a Thomas J. White, born about 1820, who died in 1876, with no unit. What unit he served in is not yet known.
- When and where did he enlist, and what did his company do? His compiled military service record (National Archives, RG 94) will say.
- Did he or his widow apply for a pension? The General Index to Pension Files (NARA T288/T289) has not yet been searched for him.
- Where was he born, and how did he come to Providence?
- Where did he live and work in Providence? The city directories and the censuses of 1870–1920 have not yet been read for him.
- Did he marry, and did he leave children?
- When was he buried here, and what does the City's interment record say? The City lot record has not yet been matched.
