# WILLIAM BATES

Co. K, 11th Rhode Island Volunteers · died 1878-11-18

## Life

William Bates lies in grave G17 of the Prescott Post lot. His stone names his unit as Company K of the 11th Rhode Island Volunteers, and gives his death as November 18, 1878. [1]

The National Park Service's index of Civil War soldiers lists Bates, William, 11th Rhode Island Infantry, Company K, private in and out, microfilm M555 roll 1. [2] The index has only one William Bates in Company K, so it does not settle which William Bates died in 1905.

A 2022 list of Civil War veterans buried at North Burial Ground gives his death as 1878. [3]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.

## What we don't know

- The PV001 list has two William Bateses: one who died in 1878, with no unit, and one of Company K, 11th Rhode Island, who died in 1905. The stone puts Company K, 11th Rhode Island, with the 1878 death. Which man died in 1905 is open.
- When and where did he enlist, and what did his company do? His compiled military service record (National Archives, RG 94) will say.
- Did he or his widow apply for a pension? The General Index to Pension Files (NARA T288/T289) has not yet been searched for him.
- Where was he born, and how did he come to Providence?
- Where did he live and work in Providence? The city directories and the censuses of 1870–1920 have not yet been read for him.
- Did he marry, and did he leave children?
- When was he buried here, and what does the City's interment record say? The City lot record has not yet been matched.
