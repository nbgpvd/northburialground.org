# Emanuel Parker

Co. H, 5th U.S. Colored Cavalry · died 1898-04-08

## Life

Emanuel Parker lies in grave J15 of the Prescott Post lot. His stone names his unit as Company H of the 5th U.S. Colored Cavalry, and gives his death as April 8, 1898. [1]

No Emanuel Parker was found in the index for the 5th U.S. Colored Cavalry (it has a James and a Richard Parker); the spelling on his service record may differ. [2]

The 5th U.S. Colored Cavalry was organized at Camp Nelson, Kentucky, in October 1864. [3] The flag on this page is Kentucky's for that reason.

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.
