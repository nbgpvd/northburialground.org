# JAMES FLEMING

Co. A, 1st New York Mounted Rifles

## Life

James Fleming lies in grave G18 of the Prescott Post lot. His stone names his unit as Company A of the 1st New York Mounted Rifles, and gives his death as July 12 of a year in the 1880s. [1]

The National Park Service's index of Civil War soldiers lists Fleming, James, 1st New York Mounted Rifles, Company A, private in, corporal out, microfilm M551 roll 47. [2]

A 2022 list of Civil War veterans buried at North Burial Ground gives his death as 1905. [3]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.
