# ALEXANDER G. S[MITH]

Co. C, 17th Massachusetts Volunteers · died 1918-11-04

## Life

Alexander G. Smith lies in grave B5 of the Prescott Post lot. His stone names his unit as Company C of the 17th Massachusetts Volunteers, and gives his death as November 4, 1918. [1]

The National Park Service's index of Civil War soldiers lists Smith, Alexander G., 2nd Massachusetts Heavy Artillery, Company E, private in and out, with the note "See also 17 Mass. Inf.", microfilm M544 roll 37. [2] The stone names the 17th Massachusetts; the index card for that regiment has not yet been opened.

A 2022 list of Civil War veterans buried at North Burial Ground gives his death as 1919. [3]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.

## What we don't know

- Only ALEXANDER G. S can be read in the surname; "Smith" comes from the PV001 list, which records an Alexander G. Smith of the 17th Massachusetts.
- The stone gives 1918 and the PV001 list 1919. The stone is followed here; his death record will settle it.
- When and where did he enlist, and what did his company do? His compiled military service record (National Archives, RG 94) will say.
- Did he or his widow apply for a pension? The General Index to Pension Files (NARA T288/T289) has not yet been searched for him.
- Where was he born, and how did he come to Providence?
- Where did he live and work in Providence? The city directories and the censuses of 1870–1920 have not yet been read for him.
- Did he marry, and did he leave children?
- When was he buried here, and what does the City's interment record say? The City lot record has not yet been matched.
