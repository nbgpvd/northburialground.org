# CHARLES H. SMITH

Co. H, 3rd Rhode Island Cavalry · died 1915-03-07

## Life

Charles H. Smith lies in grave C6 of the Prescott Post lot. His stone names his unit as Company H of the 3rd Rhode Island Cavalry, and gives his death as March 7, 1915. [1]

The National Park Service's index of Civil War soldiers and sailors has not yet been searched for him.

A 2022 list of Civil War veterans buried at North Burial Ground gives his death as 1916. [2]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.

## What we don't know

- The PV001 list has a Charles H. Smith of the 3rd Rhode Island Cavalry who died in 1916; the stone reads 1915. Is this the same man, and which year is right? His death record will settle it.
- The list has other Charles H. Smiths, one of whom also died in 1915 (1st Rhode Island Militia and 5th Rhode Island Battery). With so common a name, the unit on the stone is the best guide.
- When and where did he enlist, and what did his company do? His compiled military service record (National Archives, RG 94) will say.
- Did he or his widow apply for a pension? The General Index to Pension Files (NARA T288/T289) has not yet been searched for him.
- Where was he born, and how did he come to Providence?
- Where did he live and work in Providence? The city directories and the censuses of 1870–1920 have not yet been read for him.
- Did he marry, and did he leave children?
- When was he buried here, and what does the City's interment record say? The City lot record has not yet been matched.
