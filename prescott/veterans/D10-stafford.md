# Charles H. Stafford

Corporal, Co. D, 14th Rhode Island Heavy Artillery

## Life

Charles H. Stafford lies in grave D10 of the Prescott Post lot. His stone was photographed and read on November 18, 2026. It carves his name as STAFFORD. [1]

That settles a question this page carried for three weeks. The plot survey sheet read the name as Stratford, and this page was titled Stratford on that reading. The National Park Service index, the errata to Chenery's regimental history, and the 2022 list of Civil War veterans buried at North Burial Ground all have Stafford. [2][3][4] The stone agrees with them. The sheet was wrong.

The stone gives his death as August 3, 1910. The 2022 list gives 1911. [1][4] The stone is the better witness to a death date and is followed here; the difference is recorded and not resolved.

His company is not settled. The stone appears to read Company M, the survey sheet and the 2022 list say Company D, and the index card says Company B. [1][2][4] Three sources give three letters, and the carved letter is the least certain reading on the stone. The compiled service record will decide it.

The National Park Service index, which files the 14th Rhode Island under its later name, the 11th U.S. Colored Heavy Artillery, lists Stafford, Charles H., Company B, private in and out, microfilm M589 roll 82. [2]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.

## The stone

CHARLES H. STAFFORD / [Co.] M. Reg. R.I.H.Art. / Died Aug. 3, 1910

Photographed November 18, 2026, from the north, in low afternoon sun. The company letter and the regimental line fall partly in shadow. [1]

## The records

No records are hosted for him yet. His compiled service record and the City's interment record are both wanted.

## What we don't know

- Which company? The stone, the survey sheet and the index give M, D and B. The compiled military service record (National Archives, RG 94) will decide.
- Did he die in 1910, as the stone says, or 1911, as the 2022 list says?
- When and where did he enlist, and what did his company do?
- Did he or his widow apply for a pension? The General Index to Pension Files (NARA T288/T289) has not yet been searched for him.
- Where was he born, and how did he come to Providence?
- Where did he live and work in Providence? The city directories and the censuses of 1870–1910 have not yet been read for him.
- Did he marry, and did he leave children?
- When was he buried here, and what does the City's interment record say? The City lot record has not yet been matched.
