# James E. Johnson

28th U.S. Colored Infantry

## Life

James E. Johnson lies in grave D15 of the Prescott Post lot. The plot survey records his stone as naming his unit as 28th U.S. Colored Infantry. No date of death has been read from the stone. [1]

The National Park Service's index of Civil War soldiers lists Johnson, James E., 28th U.S. Colored Infantry, two cards: Company D, private in and out, and one with no company ("unassigned recruits"); microfilm M589 roll 47. [2]

A 2022 list of Civil War veterans buried at North Burial Ground gives his death as 1910. [3]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.
