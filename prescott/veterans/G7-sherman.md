# HENRY SHERMAN

Co. A, 11th Rhode Island Volunteers · died 1886-07-01

## Life

Henry Sherman lies in grave G7 of the Prescott Post lot. His stone names his unit as Company A of the 11th Rhode Island Volunteers, and gives his death as July 1, 1886. [1]

The National Park Service's index of Civil War soldiers lists Sherman, Henry, 11th Rhode Island Infantry, Company A, private in and out, microfilm M555 roll 6. [2]

A 2022 list of Civil War veterans buried at North Burial Ground gives his death as 1881. [3]

His service record, any pension file, and his life before and after the war have not yet been researched. This page will grow as the records are read.
